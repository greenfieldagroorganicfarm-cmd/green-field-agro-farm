About Us

Green Field Integrated Agro and Organic Farm is an agricultural initiative located in Beni, Myagdi, Gandaki Province, Nepal. Our vision is to promote sustainable farming, organic production and responsible use of natural resources.


We believe in connecting traditional agricultural knowledge with modern and environmentally friendly farming practices.

Our Farm Activities

🌱 Organic Farming

Environment-friendly farming practices with a focus on healthy and sustainable food production.

🐄 Livestock

Integrated livestock farming supporting sustainable agricultural production.

🌾 Agriculture

Growing agricultural products suited to the local climate and environment of Myagdi.

♻️ Sustainable Future

Building an integrated farming system that respects nature and supports the local community.
