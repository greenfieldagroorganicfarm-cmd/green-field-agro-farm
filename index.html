About Us

Green Field Integrated Agro and Organic Farm is an agricultural initiative located in Beni, Myagdi, Gandaki Province, Nepal.

Our vision is to promote sustainable farming, organic production and responsible use of natural resources while contributing to a healthier and greener future.

We aim to connect traditional agricultural knowledge with modern, environmentally friendly farming practices. Through our work, we hope to encourage responsible agriculture, protect the natural environment and create meaningful opportunities for the local community.
