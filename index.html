About Us

Green Field Integrated Agro and Organic Farm is an agricultural initiative located in Beni, Myagdi, Gandaki Province, Nepal.

Our goal is to promote organic farming, sustainable agriculture, local production and environmentally friendly farming practices.

We believe in producing healthy food while protecting nature and creating opportunities for the local community.
